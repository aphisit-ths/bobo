# What is Itch Log for?

**One line:** it tells you the odds you'll itch today, based on what you've eaten,
how you slept, and how stressed you are.

## The problem

If you itch a few times a week, you eventually start guessing. *Was it the beef?
The beer? That night I only slept four hours?* You can't tell, because you're
comparing today against a memory — and memory is terrible at this. You remember
the night you ate shrimp and broke out. You don't remember the four times you ate
shrimp and were fine.

## What it does instead

Every day you log three things:

- what you ate
- when you slept and woke
- how stressed you were, 1 to 5

Then one question before bed: **did you itch, yes or no?**

That's it. Twenty seconds a day.

## What you get back

After five closed days, a number appears at the top: **the chance you itch
today**, with a receipt under it showing exactly what moved it.

    baseline        43%
    ▲ beef          +18
    ▲ short sleep   +11
    ▼ spinach        −6

Every food you've ever logged also gets a line showing how often you itched after
eating it, measured against how often you itch anyway.

## Why "against how often you itch anyway" matters

This is the part most food diaries get wrong. If you itch on 60% of all days,
then a food sitting at 60% is *innocent* — it's just your normal. Without that
comparison every food looks guilty and you end up cutting things for no reason.

Two more guards against fooling yourself:

- A food you've eaten twice can't be ranked. It stays faded until three days.
- Anything you eat every single day (rice, coffee) can never separate from your
  baseline. The app says so and tells you to drop it for a week if you want a
  real answer.

## What it is not

It doesn't diagnose anything. It finds correlations in one person's diary, which
is a **lead worth testing**, not proof. The way you test a lead is to cut the
suspect for two weeks, eat it again, and watch what happens.

Real triggers are often not food at all — heat, sweat, alcohol, certain
painkillers, tight clothes, stress, bad sleep. That's why sleep and stress are in
here next to the food.

If the itching is frequent or getting worse, see a dermatologist. Bring the
export file; it's more useful than trying to remember.

## Privacy

Everything lives in your browser. No account, no server, no analytics, nothing
leaves the device. Export gives you a JSON file you own. Wipe means wiped.
